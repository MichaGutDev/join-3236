Privacy Policy

General Information

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use Join, how it is used, and where it is stored. Join is a student project developed as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH and is not operated as a commercial service.

Data Collection & Storage

When you register with an email address and password, or use the guest login, we collect and store the following information: your name, email address, and any tasks or contacts you create within the application. This data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), a backend service provided by Google.

Usage of Data

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your account and enabling secure login.
  • Displaying your name and initials on tasks assigned to you.
  • Storing and displaying contacts and tasks shared across the team.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties beyond the infrastructure required to operate Join (i.e. Google Firebase).

Guest Login

The guest login creates a temporary, anonymous account via Firebase Authentication. No personal information is required or stored beyond a temporary session identifier, which is deleted when you sign out.

Your Rights

You may request the correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below. As this is an educational project, deletion requests will be handled manually by the listed students.
